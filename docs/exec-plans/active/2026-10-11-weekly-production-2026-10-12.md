# Weekly Production — 2026-10-12

## Goal

Produce and release the next 42-item week from 2026-10-12 through 2026-10-18:
four Shorts and two long-form videos every day (12 dialogue episodes, two
complete Persuasion chapters, two measured 40-minute flagships). Before any
new upload, reconcile the 2026-10-05 week's remote gap so the channel stops
carrying an undocumented publish backlog.

  upload journal.
- No retry of the whole 2026-10-05 historical manifest.
- No change to slot times, release gates, or TTS rollout beyond what
  `weekly-plan-2026-10-12.json` locks.

## Plan

1. **Studio reconciliation (gated on owner login).** Open the signed-in Studio
   session, search each of the 24 `planned` content items by title/keyword,
   and classify each as `exists_adopt_id` or `verified_missing`. Record the
   result in this plan's Status before any new upload.
2. **Backfill decision gate.** If verified-missing items exist, decide with
   the owner how to place them: uploading 24 late items plus 42 new items in
   the same rolling window would breach
   `maxChannelUploadsPerRolling7Days: 42`. Options: (a) publish verified-missing
   longs immediately as unscheduled public releases while holding Shorts
   volume, (b) drop specific late Shorts, or (c) stagger. The chosen policy
   is recorded here before execution.
3. **Evidence capture.** Capture signed-in Studio Trends for the week and fold
   the signals into the plan Status (the JSON already locks the stable search
   anchors).
4. **Day-by-day production.** For each day in order: lock identities (done in
   the week plan), script and preflight (`scripts/elr.py preflight`), render
   with one heavy job at a time, master and pack, generate one complete
   image per cover through the browser imagegen pathway
   (`apps/worker-py/scripts/generate_image.py`, no-text background only;
   resize/format/QC only afterwards), then upload via the Studio browser
   session with screenshots, subtitles, playlist, and schedule.
5. **Daily reconciliation.** Every finished day updates this plan's Status and
   the week plan's slots with real video IDs before the next day starts.
6. **Carry-over closure.** When the 2026-10-05 remote gap is fully adopted or
   uploaded, archive `2026-10-03-weekly-production-2026-10-05.md` in the PR
   that completes it.

## Risks And Decisions

- **Browser-only uploads.** No OAuth in the environment; every upload needs a
  live signed-in Studio tab. Mitigation: one persistent session, audited
  clicks, screenshots per item.
- **Login dependency.** Studio and ChatGPT sessions both require a one-time
  manual login in the browser profiles; this is the only human step in the
  loop. Until then step 1–3 are blocked and production work that needs covers
  waits at the cover gate.
- **Rolling 7-day capacity.** 42 new uploads already fill the ceiling;
  backfill is only possible under an owner-approved policy from step 2.
- **Prior-week plan stays active.** `2026-10-03-weekly-production-2026-10-05.md`
  remains in `active/` until reconciliation closes, per the archive rule.
- **Known flake.** The episode-delivery timing tests are load-sensitive
  (recorded in the 2026-10-07 reconciliation); rerun failures before treating
  them as regressions.
