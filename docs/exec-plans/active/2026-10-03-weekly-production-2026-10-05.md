## Quality Controls

- Volume does not lower the existing script, audio, subtitle, thumbnail, or
  identity gates.
- Standard episodes remain 10-15 minutes; 40-minute flagships must measure
  35-45 minutes after final composition.
- The two daily long videos must address different viewer jobs or product lines.
- Shorts remain complete micro-lessons rather than trailers.
- If a day's second long video misses its release gate, it moves to the next
  open 08:00 slot; it is not published weak to satisfy the count.

## Capacity Decision

The previous rolling seven-day ceiling of 40 cannot hold 28 Shorts plus 14
long-form uploads. It is raised exactly to 42, with no spare upload capacity.
The Shorts allocation remains 28; Dialogue requests 12 releases and Classics
requests two.
