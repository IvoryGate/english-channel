## Recommended live description

Learn English through natural conversations and classic audiobooks.

English Listening Room brings together two ways to grow: practical English you can use in real life, and calm long-form listening that helps you live inside the language.

Choose a conversation series for your level:

- **First Steps (A2–B1)** — clear, usable English for everyday moments.
- **Daily Talk (B1–B2)** — natural conversations for work, friends, and daily life.
- **Polished English (B2–C1)** — precise, tactful English for meetings, messages, and harder conversations.

Prefer stories? **Classic Listening** offers immersive readings of timeless English literature, including *Pride and Prejudice*, with clear narration and a cozy literary atmosphere.

Listen once for meaning. Listen again for rhythm. Repeat the English you want to make your own.

New English conversations and classic audiobook chapters every week.

## Positioning notes

- Name both conversation and audiobook content in the first line so the channel's two promises are visible in search and channel previews.
- Keep Classic Listening prominent; it is a distinct product, not legacy content to hide.
- Do not describe the whole channel as an audiobook channel or as a generic podcast channel. The differentiator is the ladder from literature immersion to practical conversation.
- Playlist names and thumbnail labels must make the two content families visually distinct.
