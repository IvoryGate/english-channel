## Recommended live description

**English Listening Room helps you live with English in two ways: listen deeply, and use it when it matters.**

For calm, immersive listening, explore our Classic Listening collection: full readings of timeless English literature, including *Pride and Prejudice*, with clear narration and a cozy literary atmosphere.

For the English you need in real life, choose a conversation series for your level:

- **First Steps (A2–B1)** — small, usable English for the moments when you freeze.
- **Daily Talk (B1–B2)** — natural conversations for work, friends, and everyday life.
- **Polished English (B2–C1)** — clear, tactful English for meetings, messages, and harder conversations.

Every episode is made for listening, repeating, and using—not just collecting rules. Start with the playlist that fits your level, then come back whenever English needs to sound more like you.

New listening and conversation episodes every week.
