## Show description (YouTube playlist / show)

Welcome to **Daily Talk**, the B1-B2 English listening podcast from English Listening Room — the show where intermediate learners finally bridge the gap between *understanding* English and *actually speaking it*.

Each episode is a real, warm conversation between two friends, Ethan and Nora. They talk about the small, real moments of daily life — the awkward silence after a compliment, the confidence gap after years of "laundry listening," the moment easy English stops changing your speaking — and they do it in the kind of natural, mid-level English you'll hear at work, at a café, or on a video call. No textbooks. No "repeat after me." Just two people using the English you already recognize, in the ways you wish you could.

**Who it's for.** If you can follow most English videos but freeze when it's your turn to speak, this is your show. We assume a B1-B2 base — present perfect, basic conditionals, everyday phrasal verbs — and we stretch it into live, connected speech without ever slowing down for "level control." The goal is to tune your ear to real pace and real turn-taking, so the words come out of *your* mouth next time.

**What you'll get every episode.**
- A scene-first emotional hook that mirrors a situation you've actually been in
- A natural dialogue you can shadow, with one mid-episode "micro-pocket" that replays the key phrases slowly
- A "Word Tour" of 2-4 phrases you already heard, with honest payoff lines (no vocabulary lists)
- A single, doable behavior CTA — one small thing to try today

**How to listen.** Pick any episode; there's no order. Listen once for the flow, then again to shadow Ethan or Nora. New episodes every week. Most episodes run under 20 minutes at natural speed.

**Part of the English Listening Room ladder:** First Steps (A2-B1) → **Daily Talk (B1-B2)** → Polished English (B2-C1) → Classic Listening audiobooks. Start here if you're intermediate, then climb.

Subscribe for weekly B1-B2 English listening practice that sounds like the conversations you actually want to join.
