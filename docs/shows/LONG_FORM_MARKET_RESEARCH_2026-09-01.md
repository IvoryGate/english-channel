# Long-Form Market And Channel Diagnosis — 2026-09-01

## Decision

Launch one real 40-minute flagship every week, keep three 10–15 minute
episodes, and use the other two dialogue slots for 18–25 minute extended
episodes. Broaden the subject portfolio, but keep one identity:

> English is the delivery format; useful life ideas are the content engine.

This is not permission to stretch a ten-minute script. A flagship needs a
35–45 minute measured render, a new stimulus every two to four minutes, and an
average view duration target of at least eight minutes at T+7d.

### Transcript mechanics from two current long examples

The 37-minute Slow English episode opens with a blank-canvas image, contrasts
an automatic day with a chosen day, promises ten disciplines by 2:19, changes
section roughly every three minutes, recaps near minute 34, and ends by asking
for one chosen discipline. Its strength is predictable movement in simple
English. Its weaknesses are repetition, generic advice, and unclear
attribution to Jack Ma; ELR must not imitate a public figure or present invented
quotes.

The 41-minute Stoicism episode opens on betrayal, narrows the claim at 1:16 by
saying the goal is not isolation, promises seven lessons by 2:46, and maintains
attention with threat, historical names, metaphors, questions, and callbacks.
Its strength is emotional specificity. Its risks are sensational distrust and
unverified quotation. ELR should borrow the open-loop and changed-condition
mechanics, not the paranoia or authority theater.

## Product architecture

### Weekly recovery mix

| Lane | Episodes/week | Runtime | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Standard | 3 | 10–15 min | Fast topic tests, one human moment, one practical payoff |
| Extended | 2 | 18–25 min | Story plus counterargument/application |
| Flagship 40 | 1 | 35–45 min | Watch-hour anchor, deeper story, guided participation |

This keeps the six existing dialogue slots but raises planned dialogue minutes
from roughly 60–90 to about 104–140 per week. Production growth is measured in
finished, quality-gated minutes and ready inventory, not in labels.

After two full weekly cycles, scale to two Flagship 40 episodes only if both
pilots clear: CTR at least 3.5%, first-30-second retention at least 55%, average
view duration at least 8:00, no factual or identity violation, and acceptable
production cost per watch hour.

### Subject ownership by series

- **Series B / A2–B1:** ordinary life, gentle resilience, habits, friendship,
  bad days, and simple reflective stories.
- **Series A / B1–B2:** relationships, work and rest, self-esteem, boundaries,
  comparison, and practical social decisions.
- **Series C / B2–C1:** philosophy, nuance, moral tension, control, meaning,
  trust, and competing interpretations.

This is ownership, not a cage. The weekly slate must use at least four distinct
pillars so the channel does not collapse back into six versions of one English
problem.

## Weekly investigation method

Every Monday before topics are locked:

1. Capture three channel-specific searches and three breakout/content-gap
   examples from YouTube Studio Trends.
2. Compare at most five candidate terms in Google Trends with `geo=US`, the
   trailing 90 days, and `gprop=youtube`.
3. Record top and rising related queries; discard celebrity, game, song, or
   other false-intent collisions.
4. Find three current competitor treatments and calculate rough velocity as
   views divided by days since publication. Record runtime, chapters, opening
   promise, visual promise, and whether the channel is genuinely comparable.
5. Score each candidate: demand 30, audience fit 20, differentiation 15,
   watch-time potential 15, visual potential 10, series potential 10. Require
   65/100.
6. Refresh trend evidence after seven days and competitor evidence after 28.

Trend evidence chooses what to investigate. It never bypasses editorial,
copyright, identity, factual, or medical-safety gates.

## Immediate interventions

1. Produce the first Flagship 40 on overthinking for the 2026-09-10 Series B
   slot, because both channel-specific and broader US YouTube search evidence
   support it.
2. Use one 18–25 minute philosophy treatment and one relationship treatment in
   the same week to test breadth without rebranding the channel overnight.
3. Run native YouTube title/thumbnail tests on long videos; retain a specific,
   bounded control rather than replacing every original package.
4. Repackage `You Disagree at Work` before rewriting it: its 4:55 viewing depth
   is strong relative to the recent cohort, while CTR is only 2.4%.
5. Diagnose or rebuild the opening of `You Know “Get”`: its large impression
   test and 0:45 viewing depth indicate a content/opening failure.
6. Maintain a two-week script buffer and a one-week rendered-media buffer.
   A scheduled slot with no measured ready asset is a production warning, not
   evidence that the format exists.
