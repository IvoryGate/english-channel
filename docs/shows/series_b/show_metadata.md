## Show description (YouTube playlist / show)

Welcome to **First Steps**, the A2-B1 easy English podcast from English Listening Room — the show that gives beginners a clear, doable plan instead of another pile of textbook rules.

Every episode, Riley (your patient coach) and Sam (a learner just like you, with all the same doubts) take one small, real problem: how to practice English alone for 15 minutes a day, how to start a conversation with only three sentences, what to do when you're waiting for a speaking partner that never comes. Riley brings a simple method; Sam asks the questions you'd ask — "Is 15 minutes really enough?", "What if I forget everything?", "Do I have to talk to myself?" — and together they turn a scary goal into a tiny habit you can actually keep.

**Who it's for.** If you're at A2-B1 — you know high-frequency words and basic tenses, but real conversations still feel too fast and too messy — this is your show. We keep sentences short (8-12 words), gloss every new word immediately, and recap every few minutes so nothing slips. We never slow the speech down for "level"; we keep it natural and instead make the *content* small enough to hold.

**What you'll get every episode.**
- A clear episode contract in the first 60 seconds: "By the end of this, you will…"
- A learner-pain hook that names the exact frustration you've been feeling
- A simple 2-3 part method, labeled plainly (Part 1 / Part 2 / Part 3 — this series is allowed to be explicit)
- A mid-episode recap and one practice prompt you can do alone, today
- A light, single CTA — try it, comment how it went, subscribe

**How to listen.** Any episode stands alone — pick the problem that matches your week. Listen once, then do the 15-minute practice. New episodes every week, under 20 minutes at natural speed.

**Part of the English Listening Room ladder:** **First Steps (A2-B1)** → Daily Talk (B1-B2) → Polished English (B2-C1) → Classic Listening audiobooks. Start here if you're a beginner, then climb as you grow.

Subscribe for weekly A2-B1 easy English that turns "I should practice more" into "I already did, today."
