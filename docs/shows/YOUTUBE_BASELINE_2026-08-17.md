### Product interpretation

- **First Steps is the reach engine.** It supplies 60.8% of dialogue views and
  56.1% of dialogue subscribers. Concrete beginner jobs such as a morning
  routine, phone calls, and self-introductions earn the most distribution.
- **Daily Talk is the satisfaction engine.** It has lower reach but the best
  average duration, average percentage viewed, and watch-minutes-per-impression
  proxy. Its main problem is packaging, not a lack of useful content.
- **Polished English is a small authority bet.** It has the highest subscriber
  conversion per view, but the smallest sample and weakest aggregate
  satisfaction. Keep it at low cadence while concentrating on specific meeting
  and interpersonal tensions.

## Video-Level Signals

### First Steps

Strong reach:

- `A Simple Morning English Routine`: 263 views, 9,300 impressions, 2.3% CTR,
  2:15 average view, 18.0% viewed, +6 subscribers, and 44% still watching at
  0:30.
- `Learn English Phone Calls`: 174 views, 6,640 impressions, 2.1% CTR, 1:43
  average view, 14.4% viewed, +4 subscribers, and 37% still watching at 0:30.

Best content-depth signal:

- `Practice English Alone Every Day, Only 15 Minutes`: 96 views, 3.0% CTR,
  3:03 average view, and 30.2% viewed.

The next First Steps concepts should preserve concrete tasks and numbers while
moving the promised useful line or rehearsal into the first 30–45 seconds.

### Daily Talk

Strong content with room to improve packaging:

- `Talk About Mistakes`: 61 views, 3.0% CTR, 5:37 average view, and 47.4%
  viewed.
- `When Your Mind Goes Blank Mid-Sentence`: 29 views, 2.6% CTR, 3:52 average
  view, and 31.3% viewed.
- `Talk Out Loud When Nobody Is Listening`: 21 views and only 1.4% CTR, but
  4:03 average view and 32.2% viewed. This is a strong thumbnail-repackaging
  candidate because satisfaction is much better than selection.
- `You Said the Wrong Thing — Repair the Conversation`: 19 views, 1.6% CTR,
  3:13 average view, and 31.7% viewed.

Clear content or promise mismatch:

- `Real Talk Goes Off Script Fast`: 20 views, 1.5% CTR, 0:46 average view, and
  6.4% viewed.

### Polished English

Best current model:

- `When Fluent People Still Avoid the Hard Sentence`: 65 views, 1,840
  impressions, 3.2% CTR, 3:12 average view, 24.8% viewed, and +1 subscriber.
- `How to Jump In Without Stealing the Floor`: 24 views, 2.0% CTR, 3:09
  average view, 24.4% viewed, and +2 subscribers.

Packaging opportunity:

- `Switching Languages All Day Is Exhausting`: only 0.7% CTR, but 4:24 average
  view and 36.1% viewed. The content is stronger than the click promise.

Opening failures:

- `Give Feedback in English Without Sounding Cold`: 3.0% CTR but only 0:35
  average view and 4.9% viewed.
- `Your Story Started Too Early`: 0:53 average view and 7.3% viewed.
- `Small Talk Is Not Small`: 0:19 average view and 2.6% viewed.

These episodes indicate that an attractive premise is not enough; the opening
must immediately stage the exact social tension and demonstrate the promised
repair.

## Audience Baseline

- Watch time from unsubscribed viewers: 96.9%; subscribed: 3.1%.
- Gender: 65.5% women and 34.5% men.
- Age: 25–34 is 57.9%; 35–44 is 28.3%.
- Devices by watch time: mobile 69.6%, computer 20.3%, TV 5.4%, tablet 4.8%.
- Top reported geographies: India 8.0%, United States 7.1%, Germany 3.0%,
  Italy 3.0%, and Spain 2.9%.
- Caption usage: no captions 77.8%; English captions 19.0%.
- Studio does not yet have enough data to report monthly viewers, returning
  audience segments, audience-online times, or meaningful viewer overlap.

Implications:

- Thumbnails must be readable on a phone: one focal expression, one situation,
  and no more than a few large words.
- The core viewer hypothesis is an adult learner using English for daily life
  and work, not a school-age exam audience.
- With 96.9% of watch time coming from non-subscribers and insufficient return
  data, the next product goal is a repeatable series habit, not raw upload
  volume.

## Revised 28-Day Operating Plan

### Gate 0: repair and prevent identity drift

1. Build the publication ledger for all 57 published dialogue videos.
2. Correct Polished English 019 only after the user authorizes the remote edit.
3. Add a publish preflight that compares canonical series, episode, title,
   playlist, duration, thumbnail fingerprint, subtitle fingerprint, and MP4
   fingerprint.
4. Abort on duplicate titles with different media fingerprints, a title/series
   CEFR mismatch, or a remote video already mapped to another episode.
5. Make upload idempotent: the same artifact fingerprint resolves to the same
   remote video ID instead of creating another upload.

### Release the existing 020/021 bank as a two-week validation sprint

After Gate 0 passes, use three separated slots per week:

| Date, 20:00 Asia/Shanghai | Release |
| --- | --- |
| 2026-08-18 | First Steps 020 |
| 2026-08-20 | Daily Talk 020 |
| 2026-08-23 | Polished English 020 |
| 2026-08-25 | First Steps 021 |
| 2026-08-27 | Daily Talk 021 |
| 2026-08-30 | Polished English 021 |

Keep at least 48 hours between channel uploads and seven days between releases
from the same series. Treat T+24h as an anomaly check, T+7d as the
packaging/intro review, and T+28d as the portfolio decision. Do not produce
episode 022 until the first three scheduled releases have valid T+7d
measurements.

### First controlled experiments

1. **Thumbnail test, packaging recovery:** `Talk Out Loud When Nobody Is
   Listening`. Its 1.4% CTR and 32.2% average viewed make it the cleanest
   “content strong, packaging weak” candidate.
2. **Thumbnail test, advanced niche:** `Switching Languages All Day Is
   Exhausting`. Treat the result as exploratory because impressions are low.
3. **Intro cohort:** use future Daily Talk/Polished releases to test
   “cold-open tension → useful line → context” against the current explanatory
   opening. Hold topic class and thumbnail family stable.
4. **First Steps topic cohort:** concrete recurring tasks and numbered frames
   versus abstract confidence/motivation topics.

Do not change title, thumbnail, intro, and cadence in the same experiment. A
thumbnail variant wins only if watch-time share improves without a material
drop in average view duration or 30-second retention.

## Revised Product Allocation

For the next measured cycle, allocate editorial and production capacity as:

- 50% First Steps for reach;
- 35% Daily Talk for satisfaction and repeat behavior;
- 15% Polished English for authority and high-value conversion.

This is a test allocation, not a permanent cancellation of any series. Review
it after a complete 28-day cycle with returning-viewer data.
